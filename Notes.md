## What is predictability?

- scale of precision (where does the prediction end)
- chaos (how much do things deviate)
- timescale (how well in advance can you predict)
- depends on initial conditions

Leo and Marlene's definition

- a measure of the rate of separation of initially close states
- a characteristic of the physical system itself

"Indicates the possibility of prediction (...)" - IPCC 

(Three) types of prediction

- short term, mid term, long term, extra long term
- 1st kind: short term, initial value problem
- 2nd kind: long term, boundary value problem, initial value not to be important
- combo: seasonal-decadal climate prediction


    How good are my predictions? This depends on the system's inherent predictability and the model you are using. 

    "We cannot know the predictiability of this world, but we can try to understand the predictability of something that mirrors this world."


*Predictability*: property of the system
*Prediction skill*: property of the model

- Usually what is done is not to predict the whole system but to pick one property of the system (target index) and try to predict that. That is complicated enough.

Homework: What is ERA-5?

- In simplified terms, Reanalysis is basically a numerical model that is corrected with observations. The model spits out predictions at each reanalysis time step and then nudges the predicted state towards observations (where/when they are available). So I guess, one can think of it as two ways. The gaps in a set of patchy observations are filled with physics or the physical model is corrected with observations.

**Ensemble members**: runs of the same model but with slightly perturbed initial condition

Bit reproducibility of climate models: same model, same boundary, same initial condition, same parameters, same machine - same outcome

**Slow variation = high memory = high predictability** - but really?

Deriving predictions from low frequency variation: make a statement about the future by using a low-frequency mode of variation. The signal will have high frequency variation and low frequency variations 

Lead time: how far into the future do I want to predict? The time between the two points: 1. start of my prediction 2. when I want to make a prediction

For the purposes of the projects: lead times - months to 6 months.

**What is a climate index?**

Two types: climate modes & climate extreme indices

Commonality between these two is that they both involve spatial/temporal averaging/aggregation.
